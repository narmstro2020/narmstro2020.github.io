Why 12?

There is no arrangement that works.

Usable screen1088 px
Hazards must be100 px apart
12 hazards need11 × 100 = 1100 px

1100 > 1088. So the loop searches forever for a spot that cannot exist.

At wave_size 3 there's room to spare and it finishes instantly. Which is exactly how this bug hides in your game for weeks.

Play, change ONE thing, play again. Four changes at once tells you nothing. The reasons are the graded part — anyone can change a number.
