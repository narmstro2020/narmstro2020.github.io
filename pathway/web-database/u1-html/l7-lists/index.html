<html lang="en">
<head>

<body>

  <!-- ===== page title ===== -->
  <h1>Turkey Run State Park</h1>

  <!-- ===== L7 · the nav — one list of links, identical on every page ===== -->
  <ul>
    <li><a href="index.html">Home</a></li>
    <li><a href="turkey-run.html">Turkey Run</a></li>
    <li><a href="about.html">About me</a></li>
  </ul>

  <p>This is the park I actually know. Everything below is from walking it, not from
  the park website.</p>

  <!-- ===== L7 · an ORDERED list — the order is the whole point ===== -->
  <h2>Rocky Hollow, in order</h2>
  <p>Shuffle these and you would be walking the ladders downhill in the dark, so this
  one is ordered.</p>
  <ol>
    <li>Park at the Nature Center lot, not the inn</li>
    <li>Cross the suspension bridge over Sugar Creek</li>
    <li>Turn right into the gorge at the Trail 3 marker</li>
    <li>Climb the ladder section &mdash; both hands, no phone</li>
    <li>Follow the creek bed until it opens out at Wedge Rock</li>
    <li>Come back on Trail 5 rather than the way you came</li>
  </ol>

  <!-- ===== L7 · a NESTED list — the trails belong under their difficulty ===== -->
  <h2>Trails, grouped by how hard they are</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Easy
      <ul>
        <li>Trail 1 &mdash; paved, and the only one a stroller survives</li>
        <li>Trail 7 &mdash; flat, wooded, quiet on a weekday</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Moderate
      <ul>
        <li>Trail 5 &mdash; the sensible way back out of the gorge</li>
        <li>Trail 9 &mdash; long, but nothing technical</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Rugged
      <ul>
        <li>Trail 3 &mdash; ladders, creek crossings, genuinely hard when wet</li>
        <li>Trail 10 &mdash; Bear Hollow, and usually empty for a reason</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>

  <!-- ===== L7 · a DESCRIPTION list — terms paired with what they mean ===== -->
  <h2>Words the signs use without explaining</h2>
  <dl>
    <dt>Gorge</dt>
    <dd>A narrow canyon cut down through sandstone by the creek. Cold, and it stays
    wet days after the rain stops.</dd>

    <dt>Hemlock</dt>
    <dd>The dark evergreen growing out of the ravine walls. Left over from the last
    ice age, which is the single best fact about this park.</dd>

    <dt>Ladder section</dt>
    <dd>Steel rungs bolted straight into the rock face on Trail 3. Not optional &mdash;
    there is no way around it.</dd>
  </dl>
