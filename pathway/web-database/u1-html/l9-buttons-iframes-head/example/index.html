Welcome. This site is about the state parks in Indiana, which ones are worth the drive, and what you can actually do at each one.

Why I picked this

My family has been going to Turkey Run since I was small, and I found out there are twenty-three more state parks I had never heard of. I want to make a page for each one I visit this year.

The ones closest to Indianapolis are Fort Harrison and Shades. Both are under an hour, which matters when you are relying on someone else to drive you.

What I already know

Turkey Run has ladders bolted into the rock in some of the canyon trails. Trail 3 is genuinely hard in wet weather — that is not a warning the sign makes obvious enough.

Rugged trails, deep sandstone ravines, and hemlock groves make Turkey Run one of Indiana's most scenic state parks.

Indiana Department of Natural Resources — the official park listing

What to take

Nothing on this list is more important than the others, so it is unordered.

  • Boots you do not mind soaking — Trail 3 crosses the creek
  • More water than you think
  • A paper map, because there is no signal in the gorge
  • Cash for the gate
