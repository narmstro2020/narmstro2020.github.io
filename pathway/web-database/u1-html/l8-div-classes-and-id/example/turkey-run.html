Rocky Hollow, in order

Shuffle these and you would be walking the ladders downhill in the dark, so this one is ordered.

  1. Park at the Nature Center lot, not the inn
  2. Cross the suspension bridge over Sugar Creek
  3. Turn right into the gorge at the Trail 3 marker
  4. Climb the ladder section — both hands, no phone
  5. Follow the creek bed until it opens out at Wedge Rock
  6. Come back on Trail 5 rather than the way you came

Trails, grouped by how hard they are

  • Easy
    • Trail 1 — paved, and the only one a stroller survives
    • Trail 7 — flat, wooded, quiet on a weekday
  • Moderate
    • Trail 5 — the sensible way back out of the gorge
    • Trail 9 — long, but nothing technical
  • Rugged
    • Trail 3 — ladders, creek crossings, genuinely hard when wet
    • Trail 10 — Bear Hollow, and usually empty for a reason

Words the signs use without explaining

Gorge
A narrow canyon cut down through sandstone by the creek. Cold, and it stays wet days after the rain stops.
Hemlock
The dark evergreen growing out of the ravine walls. Left over from the last ice age, which is the single best fact about this park.
Ladder section
Steel rungs bolted straight into the rock face on Trail 3. Not optional — there is no way around it.
