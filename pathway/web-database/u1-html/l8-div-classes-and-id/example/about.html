Who is writing this

I am a sophomore at Warren Central and I have been to exactly three of Indiana's twenty-four state parks. That is the whole reason this site exists — I want a record of the rest.

Why this topic

Most park websites tell you the hours and the entrance fee. None of them tell you whether the trail is worth it. That is the part I am trying to write down while I still remember it.

Rugged trails, deep sandstone ravines, and hemlock groves make Turkey Run one of Indiana's most scenic state parks.
