Trails

Three routes leave from the same gravel lot. Pick by how much climbing you want, not by distance: the short one is the steep one.

Easy loops

Two loops of about a mile each, mostly flat, packed gravel the whole way. Strollers manage both.

The ridge walk

Four miles out and back with one long climb at the start. The top is the reason to come.

Trail rules

  • Stay on the marked path — the edges are eroding.
  • Dogs on a lead, and carry out what you carry in.
  • The lot gate locks at dusk.

Events

A guided sunrise walk on the first Saturday of every month. Meet at the lot at 6:30. No sign-up, just turn up.

Contact

Trail conditions are posted on the board at the lot. For anything else, the ranger station is open 9 to 4.
